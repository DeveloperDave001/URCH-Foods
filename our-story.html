Our Story

Rooted in Nsukka, made for the world. This is the story behind URCH Foods — and why we believe every kitchen deserves the taste of home.

Rooted in
Nsukka
The Beginning

It started with a craving for home.

URCH Foods was born from a simple, powerful feeling — the ache of missing the foods that defined our childhood. Growing up in Nsukka, dishes like Okpa, Achicha, Agbugbu, and Akidi Oji weren't just meals. They were identity. They were celebration. They were home.

But as we moved to Lagos, Abuja, and eventually abroad, we noticed something troubling: the authentic indigenous foods we loved were becoming harder to find. The Okpa sold in markets was inconsistent. The Achicha was processed without care. The Agbugbu and Akidi Oji our mothers swore by were nowhere to be found.

So we decided to change that. Not with shortcuts or compromises — but by bringing modern processing standards to the ancestral recipes and ingredients that have sustained Igbo families for generations.

Our Mission

Three pillars, one purpose.

Everything we do at URCH Foods is guided by three core beliefs that shape how we source, process, and deliver our products.

01

Authenticity

Real recipes, real ingredients, no shortcuts. Every product we make uses traditional methods — cocoyam sourced from trusted Nigerian farms, cassava processed the way it's been done for generations, cowpea cleaned and packaged with respect for its heritage.

02

Hygiene & Modern Standards

Professionally processed and packaged. NAFDAC certified. Clean, safe, and made to meet the expectations of modern, health-conscious Nigerian households and the diaspora. No compromises on safety — ever.

03

Cultural Pride

Preserving dishes like Okpa, Achicha, Agbugbu, and Akidi Oji that are at risk of being forgotten by younger, urbanised generations. We make these traditions accessible to the diaspora who miss home — and introduce them to a new generation of Nigerians.

How We Do It

From farm to table,
with integrity.

We believe you deserve to know exactly how your food is made. Here's our process — transparent, traceable, and rooted in respect for the ingredients.

1

Trusted Sourcing

We source directly from trusted Nigerian farms and cooperatives. Real cocoyam from the Southeast, cassava from local growers, cowpea from verified suppliers — every ingredient traceable to its origin.

2

Hygienic Processing

Modern processing facilities with strict hygiene protocols. Every batch is cleaned, processed, and packaged to NAFDAC standards. No open-air drying, no uncertain handling — just clean, professional production.

3

Premium Packaging

Sealed, labelled, and designed for freshness. Our packaging preserves the authentic taste and texture of each product while looking beautiful on your shelf — because traditional food deserves modern presentation.

4

Fast Delivery

From our facility to your doorstep. We deliver across Nigeria — Lagos, Abuja, Enugu, Port Harcourt, and beyond — and to select diaspora locations. Your favourite foods, fresh and fast, wherever you are.
