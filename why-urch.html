Authenticity

Real recipes, real ingredients,
no shortcuts.

We source directly from trusted Nigerian farms and cooperatives. Real cocoyam from the Southeast, cassava from local growers, cowpea from verified suppliers — every ingredient traceable to its origin.

Direct farm sourcing Relationships with growers in the Southeast, not middlemen
Traditional methods preserved The same techniques our grandmothers used, refined for consistency
No artificial additives Clean ingredients you can read and understand
Hygiene & Modern Standards

Professionally processed,
NAFDAC certified.

Modern processing facilities with strict hygiene protocols. Every batch is cleaned, processed, and packaged to NAFDAC standards. We bridge the gap between ancestral recipes and the safety expectations of today's health-conscious households.

NAFDAC certified facility Full compliance with Nigerian food safety regulations
Sealed, hygienic packaging No open-air handling — every pack sealed for freshness
Quality control at every stage From raw ingredient inspection to final product testing
