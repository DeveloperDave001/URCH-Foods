Our Story

Nigeria's culinary heritage,
preserved with pride.

URCH Foods was born from a simple craving: the authentic taste of home. We noticed that the beloved indigenous dishes Okpa, Abacha, Achicha that defined our childhood were becoming harder to find, especially for urban families and the diaspora who missed home.

Our mission is to preserve these culinary traditions by making them accessible, hygienic, and convenient — without losing a drop of their soul. Every product we make carries the promise of authenticity, modern processing standards, and the cultural pride of Nsukka and beyond.

FAQ

Questions,
answered

Can't find what you're looking for? Reach us on WhatsApp or at hello@urchfoods.com — we reply within 2 hours.

How do I order from URCH Foods?
+
Simply browse our products, add items to your cart, and proceed to checkout. You can pay via card, bank transfer, or USSD. We deliver across Nigeria and to select diaspora locations.
What payment methods do you accept?
+
We accept all major debit/credit cards, bank transfers, and USSD payments through our secure Paystack and Flutterwave integration. Your payment details are always encrypted and secure.
How long does delivery take?
+
Within Lagos and Abuja, delivery typically takes 1-3 business days. For other Nigerian cities, expect 3-5 business days. Diaspora deliveries vary by location — check our Shipping Policy for details.
Are your products NAFDAC certified?
+
Yes. All URCH Foods products are NAFDAC certified and processed in compliance with Nigerian food safety standards. Our facility follows strict hygiene protocols to ensure every pack meets the highest quality standards.
How should I store the products?
+
Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. Once opened, transfer to an airtight container and use within the recommended timeframe on the packaging. Our products have a generous shelf life when stored properly.
Can I return or get a refund for my order?
+
Yes. If your order arrives damaged or doesn't meet your expectations, contact us within 48 hours of delivery and we'll arrange a replacement or full refund. Your satisfaction is our priority. See our Returns Policy for full details.
Do you offer wholesale or bulk pricing?
+
Yes! We offer competitive wholesale pricing for retailers, restaurants, hotels, and corporate buyers. Visit our Wholesale page or contact us directly for a custom quote and minimum order information.
