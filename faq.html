Ordering & Payment

How do I order from URCH Foods?
+
Simply browse our products on the Shop page, add items to your cart, and proceed to checkout. You can pay via card, bank transfer, or USSD. We deliver across Nigeria and to select diaspora locations.
What payment methods do you accept?
+
We accept all major debit/credit cards, bank transfers, and USSD payments through our secure Paystack and Flutterwave integration. Your payment details are always encrypted and secure.
Do I need an account to place an order?
+
Yes — creating a free URCH Foods account gives you order tracking, saved addresses for faster reorders, and future access to loyalty rewards. It takes less than 30 seconds, and we never spam.
Is my payment information secure?
+
Absolutely. We use Paystack and Flutterwave — two of Africa's most trusted payment processors. All transactions are encrypted and PCI-DSS compliant. We never store your card details on our servers.
Can I use a discount code?
+
Yes! Enter your discount code at checkout. If you've signed up for our newsletter, check your welcome email for a special first-order offer. We also run seasonal promotions — follow us on social media to stay updated.

Shipping & Delivery

How long does delivery take?
+
Within Lagos and Abuja, delivery typically takes 1–3 business days. For other Nigerian cities, expect 3–5 business days. Diaspora deliveries vary by location — typically 5–10 business days depending on the destination country.
How much does delivery cost?
+
Delivery fees are calculated at checkout based on your location. Orders above ₦15,000 within Lagos and Abuja qualify for free delivery. Diaspora shipping rates are calculated by weight and destination.
Do you deliver nationwide?
+
Yes! We deliver to all 36 states in Nigeria plus the FCT. We also deliver to select international locations including the UK, US, South Africa, and other African countries. Check our shipping page for the full list.
Can I track my order?
+
Yes. Once your order is dispatched, you'll receive a tracking link via email and SMS. You can also track your order anytime from your account dashboard.
Do you offer same-day delivery?
+
Same-day delivery is available for orders placed before 12 PM within Lagos. A flat rate applies. Contact us on WhatsApp to arrange same-day delivery for your order.
How do I get diaspora delivery?
+
Select your international location at checkout. We currently ship to the UK, US, Canada, South Africa, and select African countries. Shipping costs and delivery times vary by destination. Contact us on WhatsApp for any questions about diaspora orders.

Products & Storage

Are your products NAFDAC certified?
+
Yes. All URCH Foods products are NAFDAC certified and processed in compliance with Nigerian food safety standards. Our facility follows strict hygiene protocols to ensure every pack meets the highest quality standards.
How should I store the products?
+
Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. Once opened, transfer to an airtight container and use within the recommended timeframe on the packaging. Our products have a generous shelf life when stored properly.
What is the shelf life of your products?
+
Shelf life varies by product. Okpa Flour has up to 12 months. Achicha lasts 6–9 months. Agbugbu and Akidi Oji have a shelf life of 6–12 months. Always check the packaging for the specific best-before date.
Are your products suitable for vegans?
+
Our Okpa Flour, Achicha, Agbugbu, and Akidi Oji are all plant-based and suitable for vegans. Always check the individual product pages for detailed ingredient lists.
Where do you source your ingredients?
+
We source directly from trusted Nigerian farms and cooperatives, primarily in the Southeast. Our cocoyam comes from verified growers in Nsukka, our cassava from local farms, and our cowpea from trusted suppliers. Every ingredient is traceable to its origin.
How do I cook with your products?
+
Each product page has cooking instructions, and our Recipes page has step-by-step guides for dozens of dishes. From classic Okpa Pudding to Achicha & Ugba Soup — we make cooking indigenous foods easy and joyful.

Returns & Refunds

Can I return or get a refund for my order?
+
Yes. If your order arrives damaged or doesn't meet your expectations, contact us within 48 hours of delivery and we'll arrange a replacement or full refund. Your satisfaction is our priority.
What if my order arrives damaged?
+
Take a photo of the damaged item and contact us on WhatsApp or at hello@urchfoods.com within 48 hours. We'll send a replacement or issue a full refund — no questions asked.
How long does a refund take?
+
Refunds are processed within 3–5 business days after we confirm the issue. The refund will be credited to your original payment method. You'll receive an email confirmation once it's processed.

Wholesale & Corporate

Do you offer wholesale or bulk pricing?
+
Yes! We offer competitive wholesale pricing for retailers, restaurants, hotels, and corporate buyers. Contact us directly for a custom quote and minimum order information. We also offer corporate gifting solutions for businesses.
Can I become a distributor?

We're actively expanding our distributor network across Nigeria and internationally. If you're interested in representing URCH Foods in your region, contact us at hello@urchfoods.com or via WhatsApp with your location and business details.
Do you do corporate gift boxes?

Yes! Our Gift Bundles make perfect corporate gifts for clients, employees, and partners. Custom branding and packaging are available for bulk orders. Contact us to discuss your corporate gifting needs.
